Activity 1: Digital Fort Knox

Password Power-Up!

Think of your password like the secret key to your treasure chest! Here, you'll learn how to build super-strong passwords that even the sneakiest digital pirates can't guess. Type a *pretend* password (NEVER a real one!) to see how powerful it is and how long it would take a computer to crack it.

Mission Flow
  1. Create a fake password (never a real one).
  2. Check whether it has length, upper/lowercase, numbers, and symbols.
  3. Improve it until it rates strong or very strong.

Imagine your online account is a house and your password is the front door lock. A weak password is like a lock made of cardboard — easy to break. A strong password is like a giant vault door — almost impossible to open without the right combination.

Computers can try billions of guesses per second! That’s why “password123” is cracked in less than a second. But a long, random mix like “BlueTaco!47Galaxy” could take thousands of years.

Tips for strong passwords: Use 12+ characters, mix UPPERCASE, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Never use your name, birthday, or the word “password”!

Test Your Password

⚠️ Use a MADE-UP password only — never type a real password here!
  • ❌ At least 12 characters
  • ❌ Uppercase & lowercase letters
  • ❌ At least one number
  • ❌ At least one symbol
