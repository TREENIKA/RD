Activity 3: Your Cyber IQ

Secure or Not? Quiz Show!

It's quiz time! For each situation, you'll choose the safest option. This helps you think like a cyber pro and make smart choices online. Can you get a perfect score and show off your amazing cyber smarts?

Being “secure” online is like being careful in the real world. You wouldn’t give your house key to a stranger, right? The same thinking applies online!

Secure habits include:
• Using different passwords for different accounts
• Not clicking strange links
• Logging out when done on shared computers
• Not sharing your personal info with strangers online

Cyber pros call this "security hygiene" — just like brushing your teeth, you do these things regularly to stay safe!
