About A.W. Beattie Career Center

A public vocational and technical school that prepares high school students from nine surrounding districts for real careers in technology, healthcare, trades, and more.

What it is

A.W. Beattie Career Center is a public vocational/technical education center that provides hands-on, career-focused training for high school students from several surrounding school districts.

Location & Contact

9600 Babcock Blvd
Allison Park, PA 15101
Phone: (412) 847-1900

Mission

The center prepares students to be college- and career-ready by offering real-world, practical training across a variety of industries.

Partnering School Districts

Beattie serves students from nine area public school districts including: Avonworth, Deer Lakes, Fox Chapel Area, Hampton Township, Northgate, North Allegheny, North Hills, Pine-Richland, and Shaler Area.

🎔 Who Can Attend

Students in grades 10–12 can enroll in one-half day career programs.

Students continue to take academic classes at their home high schools and attend Beattie for CTE courses.

Seniors often take industry certification exams (like NOCTI) that can also lead to college credits or certifications.

🧓 Programs & Training

Beattie offers many career pathways, allowing students to explore different industries and gain skills before graduation. Programs may vary slightly year to year, but include:

🚗 Technical & Trades

  • Automotive Technology
  • Automotive Collision Technology
  • Carpentry/Building Construction
  • HVAC
  • Computer Systems, Network Engineering & Cyber Security
  • Robotics Engineering Technology

🍽️ Hospitality & Services

  • Culinary Arts
  • Pastry Arts/Commercial Baking
  • Cosmetology
  • Early Childhood Education

🏥 Healthcare & Sciences

  • Health & Nursing Sciences
  • Emergency Response Technology
  • Introduction to Pharmacy
  • Surgical Sciences
  • Dental Careers
  • Sports Medicine & Rehab Therapy

🐾 Other Career Areas

  • Advertising Design
  • Veterinary Science Technology

🛠️ Hands-On Experience

Industry Certifications: Many programs lead to certifications students can earn before graduating.

Real Work Opportunities: Seniors can participate in Cooperative Education or externships with local businesses, getting paid on-the-job experience related to their field of study.

Public Services: Some programs operate real public services like a restaurant (Culinary & Pastry) and a cosmetology salon, giving students direct customer experience.

🏆 Student Life & Activities

Beattie supports student organizations and activities that build leadership skills:

  • SkillsUSA – a national organization where students compete and develop leadership and technical skills. Beattie students have won state and national awards.
  • Other activities include clubs like Beattie 360 Outdoors, E-Sports, and student ambassador groups.

🎯 Outcomes & Benefits

Students graduating from Beattie are prepared to:

  • Enter the workforce with practical, job-ready skills.
  • Earn college credits or advanced standing at some colleges/universities.
  • Earn industry-recognized certifications that boost resumes and job prospects.
