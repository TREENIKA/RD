Where Do We Need Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is not just for hackers in movies. It protects real people in many places you use every day.

Private vs Public: What's the Difference?

  • Private sector = businesses and organizations that sell products or services.
  • Public sector = government and community services for everyone, paid by taxes.
  • Both need cybersecurity: one protects customers and money; the other protects public services and safety.

Private Sector (Businesses)

Companies protect customers, money, and data so their services stay safe and trusted.

Banks and Online Shopping

  • Guards your money, credit cards, and bank accounts.
  • Encrypts (scrambles) data when you buy things online.
  • Detects fake purchases and blocks stolen card use.

Gaming, Social Media, and Apps

  • Protects player accounts, skins, and in‑game purchases.
  • Helps fight bullying, bots, and fake accounts.
  • Keeps photos, messages, and profiles safer from strangers.

Healthcare and Hospitals

  • Protects patient medical records and private health info.
  • Helps keep life‑saving machines and hospital systems online.
  • Prevents scams that try to steal insurance or billing data.

Tech and Cloud Services

  • Protects email, files, and apps stored in the cloud.
  • Blocks account takeovers and data leaks.
  • Builds secure systems for millions of users.

Public Sector (Government & Community)

Public services protect communities and keep everyday life running for everyone.

Government and Public Safety

  • Secures voting systems and important government data.
  • Helps protect military and national security systems.
  • Stops attacks on emergency services like 911 centers.

Schools and Colleges

  • Protects student grades, records, and personal information.
  • Stops hackers from shutting down online classes and tests.
  • Keeps school Wi‑Fi and devices safe for students and teachers.

Power, Water, and Transportation

  • Keeps electricity, water plants, and traffic systems running.
  • Protects trains, airports, and shipping from cyberattacks.
  • Guards sensors and smart devices that control equipment.

Connecting the Sectors

  • Many cyber pros work across more than one sector during their career.
  • Skills like problem solving, careful thinking, and teamwork are useful in every area.
  • As more things go online, new sectors will need cybersecurity too.

Examples of Who Uses Cybersecurity

Private sector companies

  • Tech and cloud: Google, Microsoft, Apple, Amazon
  • Banks and payments: JPMorgan Chase, Visa, PayPal
  • Gaming and social media: Roblox, Epic Games, Xbox, PlayStation, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram
  • Healthcare companies: Hospital networks and health tech firms
  • Energy and transport companies: Airlines, shipping, rail, and power providers

Public sector organizations

  • Government agencies: Local, state, and national departments
  • Public schools and colleges: Districts, universities, and libraries
  • Public safety: Emergency services, election offices, and courts
  • Infrastructure: Public utilities and transit authorities

Cybersecurity protects everyday life, not just computers.
