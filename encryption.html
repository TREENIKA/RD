Activity 6: Top Secret Messages

Secret Codes & Encryption!

Want to send secret messages like a spy? This activity is all about encryption, which is how we turn normal messages into secret codes that only special people can read. You'll use a classic code-making method (called a Caesar cipher) to encrypt and decrypt messages. It's like magic, but it's really just clever math!

Encryption is like writing a secret message in a code only your friend knows how to read. Even if someone else finds the note, it looks like gibberish to them!

The Caesar cipher works by shifting each letter by a set number. With a shift of 3: A→D, B→E, C→F... so “Hello” becomes “Khoor”!

Modern encryption used on the internet (like HTTPS) works on the same idea but is astronomically more complex, using math that would take computers millions of years to crack without the secret key.

You use encryption every day — when you log into your school account, send a message, or make an online purchase, encryption keeps your data private!
