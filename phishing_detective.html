Phishing is when a bad guy sends you a fake email, text, or message that looks real to trick you into clicking a link or giving away your password.

Think of it like this: imagine someone dressed as your teacher knocks on your door and says “Give me your homework or you’ll fail!” — but it’s actually a stranger in a costume. That’s phishing!

Red flags to look for:

• Weird email address (like noreply@am4zon-help.ru)
• Urgent scary language like “ACT NOW or your account will be deleted!”
• Links that look almost right but have a typo
• They ask for your password, credit card, or personal info
