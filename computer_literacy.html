Internet Safety & Digital Skills

Cybersecurity is more than hacking. It includes smart online habits, privacy, respectful behavior, and knowing how your devices work. These are the core skills every student should have.

Every day, millions of people fall for scams, lose accounts, or expose their personal data — because of habits, not hacking. Here's how to protect yourself.

🌐 Smart Browsing

  • Check website names carefully before signing in — one typo could land you on a fake site.
  • Avoid downloads from random pop-ups or unknown sources.
  • If a message says "ACT NOW!" pause and verify first — urgency is a manipulation tactic.
  • Avoid logging into sensitive accounts on public Wi-Fi networks.

🔑 Password & Account Safety

  • Use passphrases with 12+ characters — length beats complexity.
  • Never reuse one password across multiple apps or sites.
  • Turn on 2FA for email, school, and gaming accounts.
  • Use a password manager so you only have to remember one master password.

👤 Privacy & Digital Footprint

  • Assume anything posted online can be shared — screenshots exist forever.
  • Limit profile visibility to people you actually know.
  • Never share your full name, address, school schedule, or passwords online.
  • Apps and websites collect data about you even when you're not using them — review privacy settings regularly.
  • Your digital footprint can follow you into college applications and job interviews — think before you post.

💻 Computer Basics That Matter

  • Know what files, folders, and backups are — and keep backups of what matters.
  • Understand updates, antivirus software, and why secure Wi-Fi matters.
  • Log out on shared devices and lock your screen when you step away.
  • Review which apps have access to your camera, microphone, and location.
