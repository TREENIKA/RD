Careers in Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity jobs help protect people, schools, companies, and even countries from hackers.

Salary ranges below are rough estimates for the United States and can change over time and by location.

Cybersecurity Analyst

Typical salary: $70,000 – $120,000 per year

  • Watches networks for suspicious activity and alerts.
  • Investigates possible attacks and helps stop them.
  • Sets up tools that keep computers and data safe.

Penetration Tester (Ethical Hacker)

Typical salary: $80,000 – $140,000 per year

  • Gets paid to try to break into systems in a legal way.
  • Finds weaknesses before real hackers do.
  • Writes reports that show what to fix.

Security Engineer

Typical salary: $90,000 – $150,000 per year

  • Designs and builds secure systems and networks.
  • Chooses security tools like firewalls and encryption.
  • Works with developers to make apps safer.

Incident Responder

Typical salary: $75,000 – $130,000 per year

  • Acts quickly when an attack or data breach happens.
  • Figures out what went wrong and what was damaged.
  • Helps systems recover and prevents future attacks.

Security Consultant

Typical salary: $85,000 – $150,000 per year

  • Advises schools, companies, or governments on staying safe.
  • Reviews security plans and policies.
  • Trains people on how to avoid cyber risks.

Digital Forensics Specialist

Typical salary: $70,000 – $130,000 per year

  • Collects and studies digital evidence after a cybercrime.
  • Works with law enforcement or companies.
  • Helps explain what happened using data and logs.

How to Get Started Now

  • Learn the basics of coding with languages like Python or JavaScript.
  • Practice safe online habits: strong passwords, updates, and thinking before you click.
  • Join a coding or cybersecurity club at school if you can.
  • Try beginner-friendly cybersecurity games and puzzles online.

Cyber careers are growing fast and need creative problem solvers.
