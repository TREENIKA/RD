Activity 4: The Secret Knock

SQL Injection Challenge!

Imagine you have a secret knock to get into a club, but what if someone figures out a "master knock" that works for everyone? That's kind of what SQL Injection is! You'll try to trick a pretend login screen to let you in without the right password. This shows how important it is for websites to be built securely! (No real websites or data are involved, just a fun demo!)
